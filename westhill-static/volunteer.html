Open Volunteer Positions

We are seeking dedicated community members to join our team for the following volunteer support roles.

YOUTH HUB

Youth Hub Mentor

Support high school students during study hours: assist with math or science homework, guide computer coding exercises, and build positive peer relationships.

Apply to Volunteer
SENIORS WELLNESS

Seniors Social Facilitator

Assist with cooking lunches, hosting active board game tables, managing book clubs, or doing wellness calls to seniors in Morningside-West Hill.

Apply to Volunteer
FOOD DRIVES

Food Security Sorter

Help sort incoming local community food donations, package weekly grocery hampers, and assist with sorting fresh produce for families facing crisis.

Volunteer Application Form

Please fill out this form to register interest. To ensure the safety of our program participants (especially youth and seniors), all volunteers are required to complete a training orientation and clear a standard Vulnerable Sector Police Check.

For questions regarding community service hour letters for TDSB high school students or college placements, please contact our volunteer coordinator directly at volunteer@westhillcommunity.org.

Training & Support

We provide full training, volunteer badges, references, and volunteer appreciation banquets twice a year!
