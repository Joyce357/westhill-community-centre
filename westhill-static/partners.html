Sponsorship & Collaboration

Our corporate sponsorship levels enable local businesses and major foundations to invest directly in Scarborough-West Hill. Support helps buy educational books, coding software, kitchen supplies, or sports gear.

Community Builder Level

Funds programmatic operating spaces, laptop lab equipment upgrades, and large seasonal block parties.

Family Supporter Level

Sponsors housing counseling guides, family settlement support kits, and parent tutoring supplies.

Youth Advocate Level

Sponsors educational field trips, mentorship materials, and athletic supplies for youth hub participants.

Become a Partner

Interested in running cooperative workshops, volunteering your staff, or sponsoring a program? Send us a message and our partnerships team will coordinate a meeting.
